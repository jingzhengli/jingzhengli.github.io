Profile

About

I am an Assistant Researcher at Zhongguancun Laboratory, collaborating with Prof. Xianglong Liu. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Beihang University, advised by Prof. Wenfei Fan and Dr. Hailong Sun.

My research connects machine learning and computer vision with the safety of autonomous systems. I study how models behave under distribution shift and low-quality supervision, and how simulation, world models, adversarial attacks, and multimodal foundation models can expose and reduce safety risks.

01

Autonomous-system safety

Perception and planning safety, risk identification, adversarial testing.

02

Scenario intelligence

Safety-critical benchmarks, controllable generation, simulation at scale.

03

Robust learning

Noisy supervision, open-world recognition, transfer and domain adaptation.

04

Multimodal AI

Vision-language models, LLM ensembles, and data-efficient adaptation.
